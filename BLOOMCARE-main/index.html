WHO WE ARE

Dedicated to Safe, Genuine & Accessible Healthcare Across Uganda

BloomCare Pharmacy bridges clinical excellence with state-of-the-art digital technology. Operating from our central dispensing facility in Kampala with regional courier fulfillment across Uganda, we ensure every patient receives authentic, quality-assured medicines verified by registered clinical pharmacists.

We eliminate the dangers of counterfeit medicines through a sealed, temperature-monitored supply chain direct from NDA-accredited manufacturers to your doorstep.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

Rigorous Pharmaceutical Standards & Cold-Chain Integrity

Patient safety is paramount. Every medication dispensed at BloomCare adheres strictly to Good Pharmacy Practice (GPP) and Good Distribution Practice (GDP) protocols.

Cold-Chain Climate Storage (2°C – 8°C)

Dedicated commercial medical refrigerators with 24/7 digital temperature telemetry for insulin, vaccines, and biologics.

Prescription Clinical Gate

Prescription-Only Medicines (POM) are dispensed only after thorough pharmacist review for contraindications, dosage accuracy, and drug interactions.

One-on-One Patient Counseling

Direct consultation sessions with licensed pharmacists for therapy counseling, adverse effect monitoring, and chronic disease guidance.

Chronic Medication Refills

Automated, scheduled maintenance deliveries for hypertension, diabetes, and asthma therapy management.

2°C – 8°C Digital Telemetry Validated
