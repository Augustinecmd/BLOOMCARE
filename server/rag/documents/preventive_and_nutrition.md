# BloomCare Preventive Healthcare, Immunization & Nutrition

## Malaria Prevention & Vector Control
- **Insecticide-Treated Mosquito Nets (ITNs):**
  - Consistently sleep under long-lasting insecticide-treated mosquito nets every night, especially for young children and pregnant women.
- **Environmental Management:**
  - Eliminate stagnant water collections around residential compounds (discarded tires, uncovered water barrels, plastic containers) to disrupt mosquito breeding cycles.
  - Install window and door screens, and wear light-colored long sleeves in the evening and early morning hours.
- **Intermittent Preventive Treatment in Pregnancy (IPTp):**
  - Pregnant mothers in endemic regions of Uganda should receive sulfadoxine-pyrimethamine (SP) starting from the second trimester as directed by antenatal clinics.

## Routine Vaccination & Immunization
- **Childhood Immunization (UNEPI Schedule):**
  - BCG (Tuberculosis) and Hepatitis B at birth.
  - Polio (bOPV/IPV) and Pentavalent (DTP-HepB-Hib) at 6, 10, and 14 weeks.
  - Rotavirus and Pneumococcal Conjugate Vaccine (PCV).
  - Measles-Rubella vaccine at 9 months and booster at 18 months.
- **Adult Vaccines:**
  - Tetanus toxoid booster every 10 years or following deep contaminated wounds.
  - Annual Influenza vaccine, especially for elderly individuals, chronic disease patients, and healthcare workers.
  - Hepatitis B vaccine complete 3-dose series for unimmunized adults.

## Balanced Nutrition & Immune System Support
- **Key Immune Micronutrients:**
  - **Vitamin C:** Powerful antioxidant supporting phagocytic cells and skin barrier function (found in oranges, guavas, bell peppers, tomatoes).
  - **Zinc:** Essential for normal development and function of immune cells; reduces duration of common viral infections (found in legumes, nuts, eggs, whole grains).
  - **Vitamin D:** Modulates innate and adaptive immune responses (obtained through sunlight exposure and dietary sources like fortified dairy and oily fish).
- **Hydration:**
  - Consume at least 2 to 3 liters of clean, boiled, or purified water daily to support metabolic function, lymphatic circulation, and mucosal membrane integrity.
