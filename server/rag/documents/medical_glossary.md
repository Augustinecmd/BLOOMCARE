# BloomCare Medical Terminology & Pharmacy Glossary

## Over-The-Counter (OTC)
- **Definition:** Medicines legally authorized to be purchased and supplied directly without requiring a doctor's prescription, deemed safe for self-care when used according to package instructions.
- **Examples:** Paracetamol, low-strength Ibuprofen, Oral Rehydration Salts, antacid chewables, simple lozenges.

## Prescription Only Medicine (Rx)
- **Definition:** Pharmaceutical agents that require authorization and a written clinical prescription from a registered medical doctor or healthcare practitioner before dispensing, due to risks of toxicity, misuse, or bacterial resistance.
- **Examples:** Antibiotics (amoxicillin, ciprofloxacin), antimalarials (Coartem), antihypertensives, insulin, controlled sedatives.

## Analgesic
- **Definition:** A pharmacological agent specifically designed to relieve pain without causing loss of consciousness. Can be non-opioid (paracetamol, ibuprofen) or opioid (codeine, morphine).

## Antipyretic
- **Definition:** A medication that reduces fever and resets the elevated hypothalamic temperature set-point down to normal body temperature (36.5°C–37.5°C). Examples include paracetamol and ibuprofen.

## Antibiotic
- **Definition:** A medicine that inhibits the growth of or destroys bacteria. Antibiotics are strictly INEFFECTIVE against viral illnesses such as the common cold, flu, viral sore throats, or COVID-19. Inappropriate use causes antimicrobial resistance.

## Antiseptic
- **Definition:** An antimicrobial substance applied topically to living tissue or skin to reduce the possibility of infection, sepsis, or putrefaction (e.g., chlorhexidine, povidone-iodine, methylated spirit).

## Contraindication
- **Definition:** A specific condition, disease state, allergy, or factor that makes the use of a particular medication or therapy inadvisable or harmful to the patient.

## Prophylaxis
- **Definition:** Action, medication, or preventive treatment taken in advance to ward off and protect against disease or infection (e.g., malaria prophylaxis for travelers, post-exposure prophylaxis).

## Adverse Drug Reaction (Side Effect)
- **Definition:** An unintended, undesirable, or noxious effect occurring at normal doses used in humans for prophylaxis, diagnosis, or therapy.
